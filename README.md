# Study-Smart-AI

StudySmart AI is your personal AI-powered study planner designed to help you overcome procrastination, manage your time effectively, and achieve your academic goals. Whether you're a high school student, college student, or lifelong learner, StudySmart AI adapts to your needs and helps you study smarter, not harder.

## What Can You Do with StudySmart AI?

- 📄 Upload your syllabus and let AI create a personalized study schedule
- ✅ Manage all your study tasks in one place
- ⏱️ Use the Pomodoro timer for focused study sessions
- 📊 Track your progress with detailed analytics
- 🔥 Build and maintain study streaks
- 📅 Never miss a deadline with exam countdowns
